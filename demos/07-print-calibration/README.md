# Calibration sheet and safe assembly guides

The designated physical test uses the **184.62 mm** old ball (580 mm measured
circumference), 300 PPI and high label density. The final globe remains 215 mm.

`test-print-three-gores.pdf` has three A4 pages: a 100 × 100 mm calibration square,
then the upper and lower tiles of Gores 01–03. Measure between the square's line
centres in both directions. Print all pages at Actual size, with fitting disabled
in both the PDF viewer and printer driver. Keep those settings and paper unchanged.

`calibration.png`, `north-tile.png` and `south-tile.png` are screen previews;
print the PDF. Gore identifiers repeat above every tile. Attribution, page numbers
and registration crosses clear a 4.2 mm printer border. The gore artwork, cut lines,
physical placement and overlap are unchanged.

The full nine-page test file is generated locally at
`output/print-calibration/test-print-184-62mm/leipzig-globe-print.pdf`.
`verification.json` records its source report and hash, plus checks of image bytes,
image placement and rendered gore content against the preceding build. The old
PDF and original source build are preserved. No physical acceptance is implied.
